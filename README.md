# ClicProduct – Product Visualization Website

**ClicProduct** is a product visualization website built with **React and TypeScript**, designed for showcasing and selling **marble slabs and wooden tiles**. The application focuses on helping users visualize products in real-world environments before making a purchase decision.

---

## Project Description

ClicProduct allows customers to preview materials in different interior spaces, making product selection more intuitive and realistic.

**Transform Slabs Into Sales**  
The platform enhances customer confidence by presenting products in commonly used environments.

---

## Spaces Showcased

### Living Room
A space that defines the overall aesthetic of a home. This section demonstrates how marble and wood tiles elevate comfort and style.

### Bedroom
Focused on comfort and creativity, this section highlights materials suitable for relaxing and personal spaces.

### Office
Designed with efficiency and productivity in mind, showcasing professional and optimized interior layouts.
